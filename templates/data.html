NASA data

Vespera reads the three candidate tables from the NASA Exoplanet Archive. Browse them here or open them on the archive and export a CSV.

Exporting a table that Vespera can read

  1. Open the table on the archive and leave the default columns in place.
  2. Choose Download Table, then select "Values only (no errors)".
  3. Save it as CSV. Lines starting with # are ignored on upload.
