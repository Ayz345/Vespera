Six students, one question: which of these dots are worlds?

We are Team Vespera. We built this classifier for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025 because the exoplanet archives are full of candidates nobody has had time to check.

Our backgrounds are computer science and data analysis. The project started as a question about whether a small team could train a useful model on the public Kepler, K2 and TESS tables, and ended up as a tool that anyone can run on a fresh export from the archive.

The habitable-zone flag is the part we care about most. Finding a planet is one thing; finding one that could hold liquid water is the reason to keep looking.
